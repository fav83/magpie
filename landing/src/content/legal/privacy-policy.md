# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** January 2025

Magpie is a Chrome extension that generates AI-powered summaries of YouTube videos while maintaining user privacy.

## Key Points

**Data Storage**: The extension stores configuration locally through Chrome's Storage API, including your OpenRouter API key, custom prompts, and preferences.

**Information Shared with OpenRouter**: To generate summaries, the extension transmits to OpenRouter:
- The content you're summarizing
- Your selected prompt and model preferences
- Your API key (for authentication)

**No Third-Party Sharing**: The extension does not collect personal data and does not share any personal information with third parties beyond what is necessary for the AI summarization service.

## Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal data.**

- No user accounts
- No analytics or tracking
- No cookies
- No data sent to our servers

## Data Storage

All data is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's Storage API:

- **API Key**: Your OpenRouter API key is stored locally and never leaves your browser except when making requests to OpenRouter.
- **Summaries**: Generated summaries are cached locally in your browser for performance.
- **Settings**: Your preferences (custom prompts, selected models, font size, etc.) are stored locally.

## Third-Party Services

When you use Magpie, your requests are sent directly to OpenRouter's API. Please review [OpenRouter's Privacy Policy](https://openrouter.ai/privacy) for information about how they handle your data.
